# Challenge — Customer Revenue Concentration Analysis

## Business Problem

The business wants to understand how much revenue is generated by its highest-value customers.

Identify each customer's contribution to overall company revenue and classify customers based on their revenue contribution. This will help the business measure revenue concentration and identify customers who contribute significantly to total sales.

## Tables

- `gold.fact_sales`
- `gold.dim_customers`

## Required Output

Return:

- `customer_key`
- `customer_name`
- `country`
- `total_sales`
- `overall_sales`
- `sales_percentage`
- `customer_revenue_rank`
- `revenue_segment`

## Business Rules

- Calculate total sales for each customer.
- Count each customer's sales across all their transactions.
- Calculate overall company sales across all customers.
- Calculate each customer's percentage contribution to overall sales, rounded to 2 decimal places.
- Rank customers by total sales in descending order. Customers with equal sales must receive the same rank.
- Classify customers based on their percentage contribution:
  - High Contributor: 5% or more.
  - Medium Contributor: 1% or more but less than 5%.
  - Low Contributor: Less than 1%.
- Return one row per customer.
- Exclude customers without sales.
- Sort by `customer_revenue_rank` ascending.

Advanced / Business Analytics
